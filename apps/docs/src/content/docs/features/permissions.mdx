## Roles

Everyone in a workspace has one of four roles.

| Role | What it's for |
|------|-------------|
| **Member** | Everyone, by default. |
| **Moderator** | Staff who keep conversations healthy. They can edit and delete anyone's messages, and see banned and deactivated people. |
| **Administrator** | Full control over the workspace: its settings, people, rooms and apps. |
| **Bot** | How an app appears in chat. Only apps have this role. |

Administrators change people's roles from **Manage members**. The Bot role can't be given to a person there.

## Rooms

| Room type | What it is |
|------|-------------|
| **Open** | Any member can find and join it. |
| **Closed** | Invite-only. |
| **Direct message** | A private conversation between two or more people. |
| **Forum** | A gallery of titled posts. See [Forums](/features/forums/). |
| **Thread** | A side conversation that starts from one message. |

### Who can do what with rooms

| Room type | Create | Read | Change | Delete |
|-----------|--------|------|--------|--------|
| **Open** | Anyone¹ | Members | Its creator or an administrator | Its creator or an administrator |
| **Closed** | Anyone¹ | Members | Its creator or an administrator | Its creator or an administrator |
| **Forum** | Anyone¹ | Members | Its creator or an administrator | Its creator or an administrator |
| **Direct message** | Anyone² | The people in it | Not applicable | Anyone in it |
| **Thread** | Any member of the room it starts from³ | Members of that room | Its creator or an administrator | Its creator or an administrator |

1. Unless the workspace limits room creation to administrators. See [Workspace settings](#workspace-settings).
2. Unless the workspace limits new direct messages to administrators.
3. Threads start from a message in an open or closed room. Direct messages, threads and forum posts can't have threads of their own.

In a forum, any member can start a post. Only the post's author or an administrator can edit its title or mark it solved. Anyone who can see a post can copy its link.

Open rooms set to auto-join include everyone already in the workspace and everyone who joins later. The workspace's first room can't be deleted.

### Leaving a room

Leaving a room hides it from your sidebar. Your past messages stay where they were. The last person in a closed room can't leave it; delete the room instead.

## Messages

| Action | Who can do it |
|--------|----------------|
| Post | Any active member of the room |
| Edit your own | You |
| Delete your own | You |
| Edit someone else's | Moderators and administrators |
| Delete someone else's | Moderators and administrators |

## Managing people

Only administrators can change roles, deactivate, ban, unban and reactivate people. Moderators can see the lists of banned and deactivated people, but can't change them.

### Blocks and bans

Sabha has two ways to stop unwanted contact.

- **Blocking** is between two people. If either of them blocks the other, neither can start a direct message with or @mention the other. Anyone can block someone, and undo it, from their own settings. No administrator is involved.
- **Banning** is an administrator action for the whole workspace. It signs the person out everywhere, removes their messages, and refuses further requests from the addresses they used. The person gets an email when they're banned, and again if they're unbanned.

## Workspace settings

Administrators can tighten permissions in **Account settings**:

| Setting | Effect |
|---------|--------|
| **Must be admin to create new rooms** | Only administrators can create rooms. Members can still join and post in existing ones. |
| **Must be admin to start DMs** | Only administrators can start new direct messages. Existing ones keep working. |
| **Allow everyone to create invite links** | On by default. Turn it off and only administrators can create invite links; the links members already made stop working. |

These settings apply to apps too. Which rooms an app's bot can hear from is set separately, on the bot's page in settings, by administrators only.

## Notification levels

Each person chooses how closely they follow each room, with the bell button:

| Level | What you get |
|-------|----------|
| **All notifications** | Every new message |
| **Mentions only** | Only messages that @mention you |
| **Notifications muted** | Nothing |

Direct messages offer **All notifications** and **Notifications muted**. See [Notifications](/features/notifications/) for how each level reaches you.
