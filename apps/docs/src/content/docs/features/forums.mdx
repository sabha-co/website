A forum is a channel that shows its conversations as a gallery of titled posts instead of one chat stream. It's closer to a message board than to live chat: good for questions, proposals, announcements and anything people should be able to find again later.

Like an open channel, a forum is joinable and sits in your sidebar. Unlike an ordinary channel, a new post doesn't ping every member. The gallery shows the most active posts first instead.

## Start a post

Click **New post** in the forum's top bar. A composer opens at the top of the gallery with a title and a body. The body works like the chat composer: you can @mention people, format text and attach files.

The post's body is its first message. Replies follow underneath, like a thread.

## Read and reply

Click a post's card to open it in a side panel over the gallery. Every member of the forum can read and reply to every post.

When you reply, you start following the post, so you'll be notified about later replies. The person who started the post follows it too.

## Follow a post

To hear about replies without replying yourself, choose **Follow** from the post's **⋯** menu. Choose **Unfollow** to stop.

If you leave a forum, you stop getting reply notifications for its posts.

## Find posts

The bar above the gallery filters and sorts the posts:

- **Solved**: show all posts, only open ones or only solved ones.
- **Sort**: **Recent** (latest activity, the default) or **Newest** (most recently started).

## Mark a post solved

When a question has its answer, the person who started the post, or an administrator, can choose **Mark solved** from the post's **⋯** menu. The post shows a Solved badge, and the Solved filter picks it up. **Reopen** undoes it.

## The post menu

| Item | Who can use it | What it does |
|------|-----|--------|
| Copy link | Anyone | Copies a link to the post |
| Follow / Unfollow | Any member | Turns reply notifications for the post on or off |
| Edit | The post's author or an administrator | Edits the title in place |
| Mark solved / Reopen | The post's author or an administrator | Marks the post solved, or open again |

## Links to posts

A post doesn't have a page of its own. Its link opens the forum with that post already open in the side panel, and a link to a reply scrolls to it. Each post's link is based on its title and never changes, even if the title is edited later.

## Live updates

The gallery stays current while you have it open. New posts appear at the top, and a post's card updates its reply count, latest activity and the faces of the people taking part as replies come in. A changed title or Solved badge updates everywhere it shows.

Two small things catch up the next time you open the forum: a brand-new post can briefly appear while you're filtered to Solved posts, and if the forum was empty when you opened it, new posts appear once you come back.

## What forums don't have

Posts don't have tags or categories, and posts aren't public web pages: only members of the forum can see them.
