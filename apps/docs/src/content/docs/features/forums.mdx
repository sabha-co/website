A forum is a channel that shows its conversations as a gallery of titled posts instead of one chat stream. It's closer to a message board than to live chat: good for questions, proposals, announcements and anything people should be able to find again later.

A forum includes everyone in the workspace, and everyone who joins later, though anyone can leave it. Unlike an ordinary channel, a new post doesn't ping every member: it lights the forum's unread dot in their sidebar, and the gallery shows the most active posts first.

## Start a post

Click **New post**, beside the filters at the top of the gallery. A composer opens with a title and a body. The body takes formatted text, @mentions, #channel links, emoji and links. Posts can't have files attached.

The post's body is its first message. Replies follow underneath, like a thread. Edit the body as you'd edit any message; the title has its own **Edit title**.

## Read and reply

Click a post's card to open it in a side panel over the gallery. Every member of the forum can read and reply to every post.

When you reply, you start following the post, so you'll be notified about later replies. The person who started the post follows it too.

## Follow a post

To hear about replies without replying yourself, click the bell at the top of the open post. Click it again to unfollow. Posts you follow appear in **Threads**, in Home, beside the threads you follow.

Someone who @mentions you in a post you don't follow reaches you in Activity, but not by push or email.

To hear about every new post and every reply, set the forum's bell to **All notifications**. See [Notifications](/features/notifications/). If you leave a forum, you stop getting reply notifications for its posts.

## Find posts

Once a forum has posts, the bar above the gallery filters and sorts them:

- **Solved**: show all posts, only open ones or only solved ones.
- **Sort**: **Recent** (latest activity, the default) or **Newest** (most recently started).

## Mark a post solved

When a question has its answer, the person who started the post, or an administrator, can choose **Mark solved** from the post's **⋯** menu. The post shows a Solved badge, and the Solved filter picks it up. **Reopen** undoes it.

## The post menu

| Item | Who can use it | What it does |
|------|-----|--------|
| Copy link | Anyone | Copies a link to the post |
| Edit title | The post's author or an administrator | Edits the title in place |
| Mark solved / Reopen | The post's author or an administrator | Marks the post solved, or open again |
| Delete post | The post's author or an administrator | Deletes the post and all its replies |

A post's opening message can't be deleted on its own: delete the post instead.

## Links to posts

A post doesn't have a page of its own. Its link opens the forum with that post already open in the side panel, and a link to a reply scrolls to it. Each post's link is based on its title and never changes, even if the title is edited later.

## Live updates

The gallery stays current while you have it open. New posts appear at the top. As replies come in, a post's card moves back to the top and updates its reply count, latest activity and the faces of the people taking part. A changed title or Solved badge updates everywhere it shows.

A few things catch up the next time you open the forum: a post that gets a new post or reply can briefly appear while you're filtered to Solved posts, a replied-to post jumps to the top even when sorted by **Newest**, and if the forum was empty when you opened it, new posts appear once you come back.

## What forums don't have

Posts don't have tags or categories, and posts aren't public web pages: only members of the forum can see them.
