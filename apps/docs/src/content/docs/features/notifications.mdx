Sabha reaches you in four places:

- **Activity**, inside Sabha, lists your mentions, thread replies, reactions to your messages and messages of yours someone forwarded.
- **Push notifications** arrive on your phone or computer, even when Sabha is closed. See [Install and push notifications](/features/push-notifications/).
- **Missed-notification email** collects the mentions and direct messages you missed while away into one email.
- **The weekly digest** is a Tuesday recap of what happened in the workspace.

## What reaches you where

| What happened | Activity | Push | Email |
|---|---|---|---|
| Someone @mentioned you, or used `@everyone` | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| Someone @mentioned you in a thread or forum post you don't follow | Yes | | |
| Someone sent you a direct message | | Yes | Yes |
| A new message in a channel set to **All notifications** | | Yes | |
| A reply in a thread or forum post you follow | Yes | Yes | |
| A reply in any thread or post of a channel or forum set to **All notifications** | Yes | | |
| Someone reacted to your message, or forwarded it | Yes | | |

Turning push or email off doesn't change what shows in Activity, so it's the place to catch up when you come back. Activity groups replies into one row per thread and reactions into one row per message, and has **Unread** and **All** filters and **Mark all read**. Reactions and forwards don't add to its count.

Only administrators can use `@everyone`, and only in open channels. In a channel of more than 1,000 people, it shows in Activity but doesn't push or email each person.

## Choose how closely you follow each channel

Each channel has a bell button with three levels:

- **All notifications**: every new message.
- **Mentions only**: just the messages that @mention you. Channels start here.
- **Notifications muted**: no push or email. An @mention still shows in Activity and in the channel's count.

Direct messages have **All notifications**, where they start, and **Notifications muted**. A muted direct message still sends push; only its email stops.

You can also mute a channel from its **⋯** menu in the sidebar, or change its level under **Notifications** in its settings. **Hide from sidebar**, in the same settings, hides the channel and stops its notifications too. To see and change every channel's level in one place, use **Per-channel notifications** in your notification preferences.

You follow a thread or forum post when you start it, reply to it, or wrote the message it started from. To follow one without replying, click the bell at the top of the thread or [post](/features/forums/#follow-a-post).

Nobody is notified about their own messages, and people who have [blocked](/workspace/permissions/#blocks-and-bans) each other aren't notified about each other.

## Push notifications

Turn push on for a device in your notification preferences, under **This browser**: click **Turn on**. **Push notifications**, in the same preferences, turns push off or on for all your devices at once.

You won't get a push for a channel you're looking at right now. If you have it open on any device, Sabha knows you've seen the message.

## Missed-notification email

If you're away from Sabha, the mentions and direct messages you miss are collected and sent as one email, instead of one email each.

- Turn it on with **Email for missed messages** in your notification preferences. It's off by default.
- Choose **How often**: **Hourly** or **Daily**.
- "Away" means you haven't had Sabha open in this workspace for the past hour.
- If you come back before the email goes out, it's cancelled: you've seen the messages already.
- Anything that no longer applies when the email is due is left out: a deleted message, a channel you've left, someone you've blocked.

The subject line never names the sender or the channel. Those appear only inside the email.

## The weekly digest

Once an administrator turns the digest on, everyone in the workspace receives it each Tuesday, until they turn off **Weekly digest** in their notification settings. It includes:

- The week's latest `@everyone` announcements.
- Channels with three or more messages that week.
- A few of the latest messages from those channels.

The digest only draws on channels you're in and can read, and leaves out channels you've muted or hidden. It never includes direct messages. If nothing qualifies, no digest is sent that week.

## Unsubscribing

Every email has an unsubscribe link. Missed-notification emails and the digest unsubscribe separately: leaving one doesn't affect the other. You can turn either back on in your notification preferences.

## For administrators: turning email on

Both kinds of email are off for the whole workspace until an administrator turns them on in **Settings → Identity**:

- **Send missed-notification emails**
- **Send the weekly workspace digest**

On a self-hosted workspace, these settings stay unavailable until email is set up: set `RESEND_API_KEY` and verify your sending domain with [Resend](https://resend.com). See [Deploy Sabha](/self-hosting/deploy/#configuration).

Sabha doesn't automatically stop emailing addresses that bounce or report spam. Resend keeps its own suppression list for those.

## What isn't supported

- Email for thread replies or reactions.
- Email settings per channel.
- Snooze. The **Do not disturb** status changes your presence dot but doesn't silence anything. To go quiet, turn off push or email, or mute channels.
- Alerts for keywords.
- Replying by email.
