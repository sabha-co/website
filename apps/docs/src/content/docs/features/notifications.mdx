Sabha reaches you in four places:

- **Activity**, inside Sabha, lists your mentions, thread replies and reactions to your messages.
- **Push notifications** arrive on your phone or computer, even when Sabha is closed. See [Install and push notifications](/features/push-notifications/).
- **Missed-notification email** collects the mentions and direct messages you missed while away into one email.
- **The weekly digest** is a Monday recap of what happened in the workspace.

## What reaches you where

| What happened | Activity | Push | Email |
|---|---|---|---|
| Someone @mentioned you, or used `@everyone` | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| Someone sent you a direct message | | Yes | Yes |
| A new message in a room you follow with **All notifications** | | Yes | |
| A reply in a thread or forum post you follow | Yes | Yes | |
| Someone reacted to your message | Yes | | |

Turning push or email off doesn't change what shows in Activity, so it's the place to catch up when you come back.

## Choose how closely you follow each room

Each room has a bell button with three levels:

- **All notifications**: every new message.
- **Mentions only**: just the messages that @mention you.
- **Notifications muted**: nothing.

Direct messages have **All notifications** and **Notifications muted**. You can see and change every room's level in one place, under **Per-room notifications** in your notification preferences.

You follow a thread or forum post when you start it or reply to it. In forums you can also [follow a post](/features/forums/#follow-a-post) without replying.

Nobody is notified about their own messages, and people who have [blocked](/features/permissions/#blocks-and-bans) each other aren't notified about each other.

## Push notifications

Turn push on or off with **Push notifications** in your notification preferences. It applies to all your devices.

You won't get a push for a room you're looking at right now. If you have it open on any device, Sabha knows you've seen the message.

## Missed-notification email

If you're away from Sabha, the mentions and direct messages you miss are collected and sent as one email, instead of one email each.

- Turn it on with **Email me when I'm mentioned or sent a DM while I'm away**. It's off by default.
- Choose how they're bundled: **one hourly email** or **one daily email**.
- "Away" means you haven't had Sabha open in this workspace for the past hour.
- If you come back before the email goes out, it's cancelled: you've seen the messages already.
- Anything that no longer applies when the email is due is left out: a deleted message, a room you've left, someone you've blocked.

The subject line never names the sender or the room. Those appear only inside the email.

## The weekly digest

Once an administrator turns the digest on, everyone in the workspace receives it each Monday, until they turn off **Receive the weekly community digest**. It includes:

- `@everyone` announcements you didn't see.
- Rooms that were busy that week.
- A few excerpts from shared conversations.

The digest only draws on rooms you're in and can read. It never includes direct messages. If nothing qualifies, no digest is sent that week.

## Unsubscribing

Every email has an unsubscribe link. Missed-notification emails and the digest unsubscribe separately: leaving one doesn't affect the other. You can turn either back on in your notification preferences.

## For administrators: turning email on

Both kinds of email are off for the whole workspace until an administrator turns them on in **Account settings**:

- **Send missed-notification emails**
- **Send the weekly community digest**

On a self-hosted workspace, these settings stay unavailable until email is set up: set `RESEND_API_KEY` and verify your sending domain with [Resend](https://resend.com). See [Deploy Sabha](/self-hosting/deploy/#configuration).

Sabha doesn't automatically stop emailing addresses that bounce or report spam. Resend keeps its own suppression list for those.

## What isn't supported

- Email for thread replies or reactions.
- Email settings per room.
- Snooze or do-not-disturb. To go quiet, turn off push or email, or mute rooms.
- Alerts for keywords.
- Replying by email.
