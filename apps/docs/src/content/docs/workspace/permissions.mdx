## Roles

Everyone in a workspace has one of four roles.

| Role | What it's for |
|------|-------------|
| **Member** | Everyone, by default. |
| **Moderator** | Staff who keep conversations healthy. They can edit and delete anyone's messages, and see banned and deactivated people. |
| **Administrator** | Full control over the workspace: its settings, people, channels and apps. |
| **Bot** | How an app appears in chat. Only apps have this role. |

Administrators change people's roles in **Settings → Members**. See [Manage members](/workspace/members/#roles). The Bot role can't be given to a person.

## Channels

| Channel type | What it is |
|------|-------------|
| **Open** | Any member can find and join it. |
| **Closed** | Invite-only. |
| **Direct message** | A private conversation between two or more people. |
| **Forum** | A gallery of titled posts. See [Forums](/features/forums/). |
| **Thread** | A side conversation that starts from one message. |

### Who can do what with channels

| Channel type | Create | Read | Change | Delete |
|-----------|--------|------|--------|--------|
| **Open** | Anyone¹ | Members | Its creator or an administrator | Its creator or an administrator |
| **Closed** | Anyone¹ | Members | Its creator or an administrator | Its creator or an administrator |
| **Forum** | Anyone¹ | Members | Its creator or an administrator | Its creator or an administrator |
| **Direct message** | Anyone² | The people in it | Not applicable | Anyone in it |
| **Thread** | Any member of the channel it starts from³ | Members of that channel | Its creator or an administrator | Its creator or an administrator |

1. Unless the workspace limits channel creation to administrators. See [Workspace permissions](#workspace-permissions).
2. Unless the workspace limits new direct messages to administrators.
3. Threads start from a message in an open or closed channel. Direct messages, threads and forum posts can't have threads of their own.

In a forum, any member can start a post. Only the post's author or an administrator can edit its title or mark it solved. Anyone who can see a post can copy its link.

Open channels set to auto-join include everyone already in the workspace and everyone who joins later. The workspace's first channel can't be deleted.

### Leaving a channel

Leaving a channel hides it from your sidebar. Your past messages stay where they were. The last person in a closed channel can't leave it; delete the channel instead.

## Messages

| Action | Who can do it |
|--------|----------------|
| Post | Any active member of the channel |
| Edit your own | You |
| Delete your own | You |
| Edit someone else's | Moderators and administrators |
| Delete someone else's | Moderators and administrators |

## Managing people

Only administrators can change roles, deactivate, ban, unban and reactivate people. Moderators can see the lists of banned and deactivated people, but can't change them. See [Manage members](/workspace/members/).

### Blocks and bans

Sabha has two ways to stop unwanted contact.

- **Blocking** is between two people. If either of them blocks the other, neither can start a direct message with or @mention the other. Anyone can block someone, and undo it, from their own settings. No administrator is involved.
- **Banning** is an administrator action for the whole workspace. It signs the person out everywhere, removes their messages, and refuses further requests from the addresses they used. The person gets an email when they're banned, and again if they're unbanned.

## Workspace permissions

**Settings → Permissions** sets what members can do without asking an administrator. All three are on by default.

| Setting | When it's off |
|---------|--------|
| **Create channels** | Only administrators create channels. Members still join and post in existing ones. |
| **Start direct messages** | Only administrators start new direct messages. Existing ones keep working. |
| **Create invite links** | Only administrators share the join link, and the links members already made stop working at once. See [Invite people](/workspace/invitations/). |

These settings apply to apps too. Which channels an app is in is set separately, on the app's page in settings, by administrators only. See [Add an app](/apps/add-an-app/).

## Notification levels

Each person chooses how closely they follow each channel, with the bell button:

| Level | What you get |
|-------|----------|
| **All notifications** | Every new message |
| **Mentions only** | Only messages that @mention you |
| **Notifications muted** | Nothing |

Direct messages offer **All notifications** and **Notifications muted**. See [Notifications](/features/notifications/) for how each level reaches you.
