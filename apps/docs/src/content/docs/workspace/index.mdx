Administrators look after a workspace from **Settings**, in the sections under the workspace's name. The same settings apply whether the workspace is on sabha.co or on your own server.

| Section | What it holds |
|---|---|
| **Identity** | The workspace's name, logo and accent color, and the emails it sends. See [Name, logo and email](/workspace/identity/). |
| **Members** | Everyone in the workspace, with their roles and badges. See [Manage members](/workspace/members/). |
| **Invitations** | The workspace's join link. See [Invite people](/workspace/invitations/). |
| **Member badges** | Short labels shown beside people's names. See [Badges](/workspace/members/#badges). |
| **Permissions** | What members can do without asking an administrator. See [Roles and permissions](/workspace/permissions/#workspace-permissions). |
| **Agent** | The built-in agent: its instructions, model and connections. See [The agent](/agent/#settings). |
| **Apps** | Apps and bots connected to the workspace. See [Add an app](/apps/add-an-app/). |
| **About** | Where the workspace runs. On sabha.co, its storage, export and move. See [Export, move or delete](/workspace/export/). |

Members who aren't administrators see only **Members** and **About**.

## Bring history in

A self-hosted workspace can [import a Slack workspace](/workspace/slack-import/): its channels, people, threads and reactions.
