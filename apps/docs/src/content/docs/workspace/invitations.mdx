People join a workspace by opening an invite link and creating an account. There's no list of pending invites to manage: whoever has a working link can join.

Everyone who joins through a link joins as a member. Change their role afterwards from [Members](/workspace/members/#roles).

## The join link

**Settings → Invitations** holds the workspace's one join link. Administrators can:

- **Copy** it, **share** it, or show it as a **QR code** to scan from another device.
- **Expire this link**: on by default, so the link stops working after 30 days. Switch it off and the link works until you regenerate it.
- **Generate a new link**: every link already shared stops working immediately. There's no undo.

**Invite people**, on the Members page, opens the same link.

## Members' own links

When **Create invite links** is on in [Permissions](/workspace/permissions/#workspace-permissions), members who aren't administrators get an **Invite people** button too. It gives them a link of their own, which lasts 7 days.

Turn the setting off and only administrators can share the join link. Links members already made stop working at once.

## With single sign-on

On a self-hosted workspace that signs people in through [single sign-on](/self-hosting/sso/), an invite link still decides who may join; the person signs in through your provider to accept it. See [Invite links](/self-hosting/sso/#invite-links).

## People who were deactivated

Someone whose account was deactivated can't come back through an invite link. [Reactivate](/workspace/members/#deactivate-and-reactivate) them instead.
