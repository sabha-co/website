**Settings → Identity** holds what everyone sees of the workspace. Only administrators can change it.

## Name

The name appears on the join page, in link previews and in every email Sabha sends. Click **Rename** to change it. Everyone sees the new name straight away.

## Logo

The logo appears in the sidebar, on the sign-in and join pages, and as the installed app's icon. Without one, Sabha shows the name's first letter on the accent color.

Self-hosted workspaces can also set their icons, colors and email sender from the server. See [Branding](/self-hosting/branding/).

## Accent color

The accent color tints links, selections and highlights everywhere in the workspace. Plum is the default; pick any of the others under **Appearance**.

## Email

Two emails can be switched on for the whole workspace:

| Setting | What it sends |
|---|---|
| **Send missed-notification emails** | A recap of the mentions and direct messages someone missed while away, to members who turn it on in their own settings |
| **Send the weekly workspace digest** | A Tuesday recap of what happened in the workspace, to every member unless they turn it off |

Both need an email provider. On a self-hosted workspace, set one up first; see [Deploy Sabha](/self-hosting/deploy/#configuration). What each email contains, and how people unsubscribe, is in [Notifications](/features/notifications/#missed-notification-email).
