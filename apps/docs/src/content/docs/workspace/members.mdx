**Settings → Members** lists everyone in the workspace, grouped into administrators, moderators and members, with apps listed last. Search by name at the top. When anyone has been deactivated or banned, tabs for **Deactivated** and **Banned** appear beside **All**.

Everyone can see the member list. Only administrators can change it: click **Manage** beside someone to open their menu.

## Roles

| Role | What it's for |
|---|---|
| **Member** | Everyone, by default |
| **Moderator** | People who keep conversations healthy. They can edit and delete anyone's messages, and see who has been deactivated or banned. |
| **Administrator** | Full control: the workspace's settings, people, channels and apps |

Pick a role in someone's menu to change it. Administrators can't change their own role. What each role can do is set out in [Roles and permissions](/workspace/permissions/).

## Badges

A badge is a short label beside someone's name everywhere they appear, such as **Steward** or **Design**. Each person has at most one.

1. In **Settings → Member badges**, click **New badge**, give it a name of up to 10 characters and pick a color.
2. In **Members**, open someone's menu and pick the badge, or **None** to take it off.

## Deactivate and reactivate

**Deactivate** in someone's menu stops them signing in. Everything they wrote stays where it is.

To bring them back, open the **Deactivated** tab and click **Reactivate**. They can sign in again with their old account.

## Ban

**Ban and delete their messages** is for someone who shouldn't be in the workspace at all. It signs them out everywhere, deletes everything they posted, and refuses further requests from the addresses they used. They get an email when they're banned. It can't be undone: unbanning lets them back in, but their messages don't come back.

To let someone back in, open the **Banned** tab and click **Unban**.

For one person who doesn't want to hear from another, [blocking](/workspace/permissions/#blocks-and-bans) is enough, and needs no administrator.
