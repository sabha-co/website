The company brain is a wiki inside Sabha, shown as **Team Wiki** in the app. It holds what your team knows: decisions, projects, how things work, who owns what. The agent answers from it. Because people can read and fix every page, a wrong answer gets fixed once, for everyone.

One rule holds it together: **the agent only uses words a person stands behind.** Either a person wrote them, or a person confirmed them. Anything the agent writes waits for a yes before it can use it.

## Getting started

A new workspace opens with a channel called **Getting started** beside General. There, the agent asks an administrator four things: what the team does, who it does it for, how it earns money, and how its work is organised. Then it picks a template and drafts the first pages from the answers.

| Template | For |
|---|---|
| **Product company** | A company that builds and sells its own product. |
| **Services or consultancy** | A firm that does work for clients: a consultancy, an agency or a studio. |
| **Internal team** | A team inside a larger organisation, such as an engineering or platform team. |
| **Nonprofit** | A nonprofit or charity that runs programs with partners and funders. |
| **Research lab** | A research group or lab running studies and publishing results. |
| **Starter** | Anyone trying out the wiki and the agent, or a team no other template fits. |

The drafts appear as cards under the agent's replies, so you read, fix and confirm them without leaving the channel. Setup is done once someone has confirmed the mission page and the how-we-work page. The channel then archives itself, and its history stays readable.

To change the template later, use **Change template** in **Settings → Agent**.

## Confirm what it writes

When the agent drafts a page or changes one, a card appears under its reply. A new page shows whole; a change to a confirmed page shows the lines it changed.

- **Confirm** accepts it as written. The agent answers from it from now on.
- **Edit** opens the words in place, so you can fix them before they're used. Saving your version counts as confirming it.
- **Not now** declines a change and puts the confirmed version back. New pages have no **Not now**, since there's no earlier version to go back to.

Anyone in the workspace can confirm, edit or decline. The card then records who did it, and when.

The agent knows a page it wrote is waiting, so it won't write a second page on the same subject. But it can't quote a word of that page until someone confirms it.

**To review**, in the wiki, lists every page waiting for a person, oldest first. **Settings → Agent** shows how many are waiting.

## Read and fix pages

Open **Wiki** from the navigation, or follow a link under one of the agent's answers. Pages are grouped into sections, and the wiki has its own search.

- **Edit** any page. **Save and confirm** saves your words and confirms them in one step. You can add a line saying what changed, and why.
- **History** keeps every version of a page, with who wrote it. **Restore this version** brings back any earlier one.
- **Changes** lists what's happened across the whole wiki, newest first.

Any member can edit a page. Only administrators create new ones; the agent proposes new pages for everyone else.

## Take it with you

**Download as markdown**, in **Settings → Agent**, exports the whole wiki as markdown files. The company brain is your team's, in a format any editor can open.
