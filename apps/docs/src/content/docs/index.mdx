Sabha is open-source team chat: rooms, threads, direct messages and forums, made for small teams who spend their workday in it. You can run it on your own server or use it on [sabha.co](https://sabha.co).

These docs cover three things.

## Self-hosting

Put Sabha on a server you control, give it your name and colors, and connect it to the login your team already uses.

- [Deploy Sabha](/self-hosting/deploy/) with Kamal, then keep it updated and backed up.
- [Branding](/self-hosting/branding/): your name, icons and colors.
- [Single sign-on](/self-hosting/sso/) through your own app.

## Apps

Connect your own code, your tools and AI agents. Each app appears in chat as its own bot user.

- [Apps](/apps/): what an app can do, and the three ways to connect one.
- [Add an app](/apps/add-an-app/): create it, choose its channels and decide what it may do.
- [Build an app](/apps/quickstart/): post a message, then listen for mentions and reply.
- [Incoming webhooks](/apps/incoming-webhooks/) for CI, cron and monitoring.
- [Bring an AI agent](/apps/ai-agents/) such as OpenClaw or Hermes.

## Features

How the parts of Sabha work, for the people using them every day.

- [Forums](/features/forums/) for longer, titled discussions.
- [Roles and permissions](/features/permissions/): who can do what.
- [Notifications](/features/notifications/): Activity, push and email.
- [Install and push notifications](/features/push-notifications/) on your phone or computer.
- [Import from Slack](/features/slack-import/).
