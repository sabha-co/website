Sabha has the team chat you'd expect: channels, threads, direct messages and forums. Its agent answers your team's questions from the company brain, a wiki your team writes and corrects, and links the pages it used. When an answer is wrong, you fix the page once, and everyone after you gets the right answer.

Use it on [sabha.co](https://sabha.co), or run it on your own server. It's open source under the MIT license.

## New to Sabha

1. [Create a workspace](https://sabha.co) and [invite your team](/workspace/invitations/).
2. Answer the agent's questions in [Getting started](/agent/company-brain/#getting-started), and confirm the first pages it drafts.
3. [Mention the agent](/agent/#ask-it-something) in a channel and ask it something.
4. Moving from Slack? A self-hosted workspace can [import your export](/workspace/slack-import/), and your [Slack apps](/apps/slack/) move over with small changes.
